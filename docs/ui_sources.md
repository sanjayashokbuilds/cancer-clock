# Interface sources

The map uses real boundaries, roads and terrain; hospital markers represent England trusts only. `web/public/assets/england-boundary.geojson` contains ONS country E92000001 from [Countries (December 2024) Boundaries UK BGC](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/c0ebe11c-0c81-4eed-81b3-a0394d4116a9/countries-december-2024-boundaries-uk-bgc1), downloaded 24 October 2026. The [ONS feature service](https://services1.arcgis.com/ESMARspQHYMw9BZ9/arcgis/rest/services/Countries_December_2024_Boundaries_UK_BGC/FeatureServer/0) supplied WGS84 GeoJSON with a 0.003-degree display generalisation and five decimal places. It includes islands; coordinates are longitude, latitude.

Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2024. [Licence and attribution requirements](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/licences).

The dark basemap bundles `uk-context.geojson`: the same ONS service for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, plus [Natural Earth 1:10m countries, v5.1.1](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/) for Ireland and nearby European context. `uk-roads.geojson` contains 735 clipped [Natural Earth roads, v5.0.0](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/roads/). Both use WGS84 longitude/latitude and cover 49°N–59°N. Country context extends from 11°W to 8°E so its cropped eastern edge stays outside the initial view; roads extend from 11°W to 4°E. Both use 0.003° display simplification; collapsed rings are removed after coordinate rounding. They are reference geography, not hospital catchments or a risk heatmap.

`uk-relief.png` is a 1000×1141 Web Mercator crop of [Natural Earth 1:50m shaded relief, v3.2.0](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-shaded-relief/), derived from SRTM Plus elevation. Its image edges correspond to 11°W–4°E and 49°N–59°N; bilinear resampling preserves the source terrain. Natural Earth vectors and rasters are [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

[Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) is bundled locally as `InterVariable.woff2` from the official distribution, with its original SIL Open Font License in `Inter-LICENSE.txt`. The earlier IBM Plex assets remain available under `IBM-Plex-LICENSE.txt`. Fonts, boundaries, roads and relief load locally; the app does not fetch fonts or map tiles at runtime.

The national headline is read from `data/processed/backlog_national.parquet`: **17,841** at **week ending 30 August 2026**, from the NHS England weekly backlog workbook. This is a published snapshot, not a live count. It includes people still on an urgent suspected-cancer pathway, including those without a confirmed diagnosis.

Use **“The 62-day target has not been met since December 2015.”** December 2015 was the last month it was met; it was not itself a missed month. The [government's 2024 NHS investigation, technical annex, section III.12.16](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/66e1b517dd4e6b59f0cb2553/Independent-Investigation-of-the-National-Health-Service-in-England-Technical-Annex.pdf) records this history; [Cancer Research UK's 12 October 2026 update](https://news.cancerresearchuk.org/2026/10/12/cancer-waiting-times-latest-updates-and-analysis/) continues to report it. Referral routes covered by the 62-day standard expanded in October 2023.

The landing-page layout, clock and embedded Bricolage / Instrument fonts come from the user-supplied `index (16).html`. The headline total is connected to the existing national snapshot; the clock is explicitly illustrative. Its original map and model logic are not used.
