# Recording the guided story

Designed for 1440×900 at 100% zoom. The dark landing introduces the 62-day
target and December 2015; its animation replays when you return. The England
count and date come from the snapshot. The clock is illustrative.

1. Select **Take me to Cancer Clock**, then search for Oxford.
2. Selection zooms the map to the hospital, then scrolls to its chart-led outlook.
3. Show the 4/8/12-week forecast and the radiotherapy pressure signal.
4. Open **Test a recovery plan**, add a five-day doctors' strike, choose Treatment
   and compare 0%, 5%, 10% and 20%. Outcomes now cover 12 weeks.
5. Open **How this works** for assumptions and the distinction between forecast
   level uncertainty and uncertainty in the simulated change.

The no-change plan matches the statistical forecast by construction. A selected
pressure changes both comparison paths; capacity changes only the plan. The
simulator estimates differences, so its old absolute level cannot contradict
the forecast headline. [Method and exclusions](recovery_plan_method.md).

Unavailable plans have a short explanation and **Try Oxford instead**. This
switches hospitals explicitly. Weak calibration or capacity identification is
still withheld; a missing forecast cannot be replaced with an invented value.

The map keeps bounded zoom/pan and small grouped markers. Coral means a rounded
estimated rise, even one person; it is not a severity or certainty measure.
All current trusts remain searchable. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Clinical review remains pending. This is a scroll-based story, not a completed
automatic narrated onboarding. Stage 6 allocation and Stage 11 voice remain skipped.
